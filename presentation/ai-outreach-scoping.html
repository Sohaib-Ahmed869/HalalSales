The opportunity

Outreach quality scales with attention.
Attention doesn't scale.

Where revenue leaks today

  • Dormant accounts go cold silently — nobody notices a buyer stopped ordering until the quarter closes.
  • Incomplete records rot — a missing email or dead number means that account is simply unreachable.
  • Promotions are ad-hoc — campaigns reach whoever a rep happens to remember.
  • Follow-up is the first thing dropped when the team is busy.

Three things make it work

  • Event-driven, not blast-driven. Agents react to real signals — a quiet account, a hot reply, a launched promo — so every message has a reason.
  • Grounded in your data. Agents read & write SAP, HubSpot and your customer DB live. No parallel spreadsheet, no stale copy.
  • Human-in-the-loop by design. Pricing, big accounts, anything sensitive — the agent drafts, a person approves.
● Foundation live today

Your platform already runs an event-driven orchestrator, WhatsApp + email agents, and SAP/HubSpot sync. We're extending a working spine — not starting from zero.

Customer segments

Four kinds of customer. Four different plays.

🟢

Active

ordering recently

Buying on cadence. Goal: protect & grow — timely reorders, basket upsell, never let them drift.

🟡

Dormant / Dead

gone quiet

Once bought, now silent. Goal: reactivate with a relevant reason — win-back offer, "we miss you", new range.

🔵

Unreachable

missing contact data

The record exists but the email/phone is missing or dead. Goal: find & verify a real contact first, then engage.

🟣

New / Prospect

just arrived

Fresh lead or first-time buyer. Goal: fast welcome, qualify, onboard into SAP, and convert to a repeat account.

Routing signal: last activity date·contact completeness·lifecycle status·order history
